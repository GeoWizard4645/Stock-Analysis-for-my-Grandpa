About

A daily stock analysis workbook, built from free public data and published automatically. The spreadsheet keeps the exact layout of the hand-built original and adds a dashboard, a sector rotation map, a signals tab and a plain-English guide to every column.

Indicators: EMA 8 / 21 · DMA 50 / 100 / 200 · Bollinger 20, 2 · MACD 12 / 26 / 9 · Volume MA 20 · RSI 14 · ATR 14
